About

Walt Corona is a composer creating cinematic and atmospheric music for video games. Drawing from years as a musician and keyboardist, he blends emotional piano, ambient textures, and immersive soundscapes to support storytelling and gameplay. His work focuses on memorable themes, evolving moods, and music that helps bring virtual worlds to life.
